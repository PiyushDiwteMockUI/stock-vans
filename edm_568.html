Why This Full-Time Family Chose Wonderland

RV Caravan | XTR Handover


Handover day is our favourite day. In this video we hand over a brand new XTR to Doug and Jess, a family who travel Australia full-time with three little ones.


This is their second custom build, and their first with Wonderland RV. They talk through why they made the switch: meeting owners in Tassie, seeing so many on the road, the off-grid capability they need for full-time travel, and the inclusions that come standard on the XTR. Jess's favourite is the layout, refined from their first build and, in her words, knocked out of the park this time. Doug's is the attention to detail, right down to the stone guard on the toolbox.


First stop, the Grampians, then straight up the middle.
