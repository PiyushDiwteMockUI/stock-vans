# Tilted Frame — the Wonderland RV EDM photo treatment
Photographs in Wonderland RV emails never sit flat. The print is laid down by hand,
a few degrees off square, over a straight slab of house orange (#DB7627) that shows
along the bottom edge. The slab is level; the photo leans. Square corners, no shadows,
no borders. The effect reads as a photo placed on the workbench of the factory it shows:
precise, physical, unmistakably Wonderland RV. Replicated from the September 2026
newsletter's community photo (AC asset 9af05cdd).
